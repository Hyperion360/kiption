Added: a config-driven footer legal line (`footer_copyright`, `footer_copyright_url`, `footer_links` in config.php; `KIP_FOOTER_COPYRIGHT`, `KIP_FOOTER_COPYRIGHT_URL`, `KIP_FOOTER_LINKS` as JSON overrides): a copyright holder and legal links render as one `foot-legal` paragraph after the foot-nav, absent when unconfigured, escaped, identical bytes for every visitor. Only https and site-relative URLs link (the nav stance); `javascript:`, `http:`, and `//host` values render their labels as plain text. The year is the render date, so a cached page carries its build year until the next build or purge. README's Operating the archive section documents the keys with the flagship example values.
