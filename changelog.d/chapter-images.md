### Added
- Authors can add pictures to chapters. The site owner turns on "Chapter images" in Settings, and then the chapter editor can upload PNG, JPEG, WebP, and GIF files up to 2 MiB each to the author's own library. Readers see the pictures on the web; downloads and feeds show the description text instead.
- The chapter editor saves over a retry-safe request, so a dropped connection never creates a second copy of a chapter.
