### Changed
- Each row in the review queue says how long ago it was sent, who wrote it, how long it is and what is known about the author. A line of keyboard hints (J and K to move, A to approve, R to remove) shows when scripts are on, and the keys never fire while you type.
- The queue's detail pane shows the author's history in three figures and links to the whole story. Remove opens a reason panel with ready-made reasons to pick from and a box to edit the words.
- Deleting a member opens a card that names who is going, lists their email, stories, open reports and join date, and offers the two choices for their stories as cards. The red button says who it deletes.
