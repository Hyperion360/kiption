### Added
- Word (.doc) joins HTML and EPUB as a whole-work download format (the same standalone document served under Word headers).
- Word-count browse chips now cover the whole range: Under 10k, 10k to 50k, Over 50k (plus the existing complete/in-progress filters).
- Series covers: owners and admins upload a cover image on the series edit form (the story-cover validation chain: PNG/JPG/WEBP/GIF, max 2 MiB); the series page renders it, the admin image library refuses deletion of in-use files, and EPUB-style purges keep the cached series page fresh.
