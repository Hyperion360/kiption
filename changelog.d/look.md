### Added
- Site owners can now change the look of the archive. A new "Colors and Layout" page in the admin menu lets you pick an accent, background, and text color, and choose a reading width, text size, density, and header style. A night palette is worked out from your colors, and a preview shows both before you save.
- Colors that would make text hard to read are refused with a plain message that shows the contrast.
- A Reset to Default button returns the built-in look.
