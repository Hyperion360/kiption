### Changed
- The admin menu folds: only the group you are in stays open, and a folded group shows how many things are waiting inside it.
- The admin home lists what is waiting, one row per kind of work, each with its own button.
- The review queue shows one tab at a time with a list and a detail pane. Remove opens its reason in place, with ready-made reasons to pick from.
- The member list has filters with counts, searches by email as well as pen name, and sets a role from a select. Each account shows one plain status, and Lock and Delete sit in a small menu.
- Deleting a member shows the facts first, then what happens to their stories.
